# TOPOFFER legal review checklist

The in-app Privacy Policy, Terms of Use, and Cookie Policy are product templates, not legal advice. Before production launch, qualified counsel for every launch jurisdiction should review and approve them.

Counsel should confirm:

- The legal operator name, physical/contact address, governing law, venue, and effective date.
- GDPR/UK GDPR lawful bases, controller/processor roles, retention periods, subprocessors, international transfers, and data-subject request procedure.
- Applicable US state privacy disclosures and opt-out requirements, including “sale” or “sharing” definitions.
- Cookie/SDK inventory, consent categories, withdrawal controls, consent logs, and regional consent-mode behavior.
- Affiliate-link, sponsored-placement, pricing, merchant, and user-generated-content disclosures.
- DMCA/copyright notice-and-takedown process and designated-agent details where applicable.
- Eligibility/age limits, prohibited content, moderation rights, repeat-infringer and account enforcement policies.
- Warranty disclaimers, liability limits, indemnity, dispute resolution, and consumer-law exceptions.
- Security incident notification, deletion timelines, backups, and law-enforcement request handling.
- Accessibility statement and a monitored contact route for support and legal requests.

Do not present the current templates as counsel-approved until that review is completed and the placeholder support channel is replaced with a monitored address.
